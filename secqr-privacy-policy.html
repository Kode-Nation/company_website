SecQR QR Scanner Privacy Policy

SecQR QR Scanner Mobile App

Effective Date: December 24, 2025
Company: KodeNation

KodeNation (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your trust. This Privacy Policy explains how our QR Code Scanner app (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Analytics, and Firebase: We use these third-party services to improve app stability, measure engagement, and serve ads. These services may collect certain device or usage information as per their own privacy policies.
  • URLs Submitted by Users: When you scan a QR code containing a URL, the URL is processed and stored on our backend servers.
    • These URLs are stored indefinitely.
    • URLs are not linked to any personal information, device identifiers, or user accounts.
  • Customer Support Emails: If you contact us at info@kodenation.com, we retain your emails indefinitely for support and record-keeping purposes.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) directly through our source code.
  • We do not require login or social media authentication.
  • We do not provide chat features.
  • We do not outsource operations; all work is done in-house.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All stored URLs are kept in encrypted databases.
  • Since we do not collect PII, there is no risk of loss or theft of personal information through our app.
  • We cannot attribute any specific URL to a particular user or device.

4. Use of Information

  • Analytics: We may use Google Analytics data to measure customer engagement and improve retention strategies.
  • Legal Compliance: Although we collect minimal information, we may use or disclose data if required by law.
  • Third-Party Services: AdMob and other integrated services may store information on their servers according to their own policies.

5. Age Requirement

The App is intended for users aged 8 years and above.

6. Response Time for Data Queries

If you contact us regarding personal information-related queries, please allow up to one month for a response.

7. Permissions Used by the App

To provide core functionality, the App requires the following permissions:

  1. CAMERA – To scan QR codes and barcodes.
  2. VIBRATE – To provide haptic feedback when a scan is successful.
  3. READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – To scan QR codes from saved images in your gallery.
  4. WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – To save QR code images when “Save Actual Code” is enabled.
  5. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – To check network connectivity for backend URL security scans.
  6. BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE – To allow adding a custom tile in Quick Settings for faster access.
  7. Prevent Phone from Sleeping – To keep the device awake during scanning operations.

8. Data Sharing and Transfers

  • We do not transfer personal information ourselves.
  • Third-party services (e.g., AdMob, Firebase, Crashlytics, Google Analytics) may store or process data on their servers.

9. Company Information

  • KodeNation is a single, independent company.
  • Contact Email: info@kodenation.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised “Effective Date” at the top of this page.
